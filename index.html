Anything else? Ask on the call, or try the chat assistant in the corner.

Do I own my website?

Yes. The domain, the site and the payment account are all in your name. We're added as a collaborator so we can help, and you can remove us at any time.

What do I need to provide?

Your services and prices, your hours, a few photos and a short bio. One intake form covers it all, and we can help with writing if you'd rather not.

I already use a booking app. Can I keep it?

Usually, yes. We can link or embed the tool you already use, so your clients get a better site without you changing how you work.

Are there other monthly costs?

Your website platform charges its own subscription (typically $29-39/month) and payment processors take a small fee per transaction. You pay those directly. Our care plan is optional.

How long does it take?

Most Booking Sites launch in 3-4 weeks once we have your content. The biggest delay is usually photos, so we'll give you a simple checklist up front.
