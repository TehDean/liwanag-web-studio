Do I own my website?

Yes. The domain, the site and the payment account are all in your name. We're added as a collaborator so we can help, and you can remove us at any time.

What do I need to provide?

Your services and prices, your hours, a few photos and a short bio. One intake form covers it all, and we can help with writing if you'd rather not.

I already use a booking app. Can I keep it?

Usually, yes. We can link or embed the tool you already use, so your clients get a better site without you changing how you work.

Can't I just make a site with AI or a website builder?

You can get a good-looking page that way in minutes. Making it work for your business is the part that takes time: a contact form that actually reaches you, your web address and Google listing set up, and, if you take bookings, a calendar, deposits and reminders that run on their own. We set all of that up, keep it working, and you can still change your prices from your phone.

What's in the care plan?

$125/month, month to month. Your site stays yours either way.

  • Your chat assistant, answering customer questions 24/7 and sending people to booking, plus a monthly summary of what customers asked.
  • Up to 4 small changes a month (hours, prices, photos, wording) or 1 medium change (a new section, service or page).
  • A monthly test of your booking, forms and payments. You'll see a test booking appear and get cancelled; that's us checking.
  • Your Google listing kept in sync with your site, so your hours and booking link are always right.
  • Urgent help the same business day: if booking or payments stop working, we start on it the same day you tell us and keep you posted until it's fixed. Other questions get a reply within 2 business days.

Not included: big new features or a redesign (quoted separately), your website platform plan and card fees, and ads or blog writing. Cancel any time with 30 days' notice.

Are there other monthly costs?

Your website platform charges its own subscription (typically $29-39/month) and payment processors take a small fee per transaction. You pay those directly. Text reminders come out of the plan's monthly text allowance (about 50 a month on the basic plan); busy businesses can add a text pack. Our care plan is optional.

How long does it take?

Most Booking Sites launch in 1-2 weeks once we have your content. The biggest delay is usually photos, so we'll give you a simple checklist up front.
