Do you handle allergies and dietary needs?

Yes. Tell us in the request and every menu is adjusted. Nut-free, gluten-free and vegan options are always available.

What's the minimum order?

$350 or 10 guests, whichever is more. Smaller orders may be possible for pickup.

How far do you deliver?

Within 20 miles of Montclair is included. Beyond that, a delivery fee is quoted up front.

What if I need to cancel?

Deposits are refundable up to 14 days before the event, and transferable to a new date after that.
