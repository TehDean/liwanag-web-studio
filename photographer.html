What should I wear?

Solid colors and comfortable fits photograph best. Your prep guide has examples for each session type.

Can I get more images?

Yes. Extra edited images are $15 each, or buy the full gallery from your online proofing page.

What if it rains?

Outdoor sessions move to the studio or a new date at no charge. We'll text you the day before if the forecast looks bad.

Can I use the photos for my business?

Brand & Headshot sessions include commercial use. Other sessions are for personal use; ask if you need more.
