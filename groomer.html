Good questions

How long does a groom take?

Usually 1.5 to 3 hours depending on size and coat. We text you the moment your dog is ready.

What vaccines do you need?

A current rabies certificate. You can upload it right after booking.

My dog is nervous. Can you help?

Yes. Book the first slot of the day when it's quiet, and note it in the form. We go slow and take breaks.
