Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

In short: we collect only what you choose to send us (a form, a booking or a chat message), we use it to answer you and run our business, and we never sell it.

This policy covers the website of Liwanag Web Studio LLC, a New Jersey limited liability company ("we," "us"). It explains what information we collect when you visit, fill out a form, book a call or use the chat assistant, and what we do with it. It does not cover the websites we build for our clients; each client has its own privacy policy.

What we collect

When you…We collect
Fill out the contact form or book a free callYour name, email, phone number if you give it, your business name and type, and your message.
Chat with the assistantThe messages you type, the assistant's replies, the page you were on, the time, and a random ID your browser creates so the assistant can follow the conversation. Please don't share sensitive details (like payment information) in the chat.
Email or call usWhatever you include in your email or tell us on the call.
Simply visit the siteBasic technical information that our hosting and service providers log automatically, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, schedule and hold your call, and send you a quote if you ask for one.
  • To run the chat assistant and to see which questions people ask, so we can improve our answers and our website.
  • To keep records for our business and taxes, and to protect the site from abuse.

We don't sell your information or share it for advertising. If we ever send marketing emails, every one will include an easy way to unsubscribe.

Who helps us run the site

We use a few service providers, who handle your information only to provide their service to us:

  • Wix hosts our website and stores form submissions and bookings.
  • Supabase stores chat assistant conversations.
  • Anthropic provides the AI model that writes the assistant's replies. Your chat messages are sent to it to generate each answer.
  • Google provides our email and the fonts on this site.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or someone's safety.

Cookies and browser storage

The chat assistant saves a random ID in your browser's storage so it can recognize your conversation and limit spam. It doesn't identify you by name. Our hosting provider may also use cookies needed to run the site. We don't use advertising cookies. You can clear your browser's storage at any time; the assistant will simply start a new conversation.

How long we keep it

  • Chat conversations: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Form submissions, bookings and emails: as long as needed to work with you, and for clients, as long as needed for our business and tax records.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we have about you, or to stop contacting you. Email us at the address below and we'll respond within 30 days. We'll keep only what the law requires us to keep.

Children

This site is for business owners. It isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Security

We use reputable providers and limit who can access your information. No website is perfectly secure, so please don't send highly sensitive information through the site.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. Big changes will be noted on this page.
