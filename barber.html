Questions

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, when a chair is free. Booked clients go first, so booking online is the surest way to skip the wait.

Why do you need a card to book?

It only holds your spot. You pay in the shop as usual. If you don't show and don't cancel, we charge $15 so your barber isn't left with an empty chair.

Is there parking?

Free street parking on Broad St and a municipal lot one block away.
